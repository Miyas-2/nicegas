Act as an Expert Frontend Developer, UI/UX Designer, and Copywriter. Your task is to design and develop a high-converting, modern landing page using Next.js and Tailwind CSS for "NICEGAS", a Bio-CNG (Compressed Natural Gas) alternative energy product developed by Institut Teknologi Nasional (ITENAS) Bandung.

### 1. DESIGN SYSTEM & VISUAL DIRECTION

- **Theme:** "Collaboration, Innovation, Impact." Tech-savvy, clean, and eco-friendly.
- **Visual Style:** 2D Vector / Flat-design illustrations. The aesthetic should feel modern, innovative, and approachable.
- **Color Palette:**
  - Primary/Dominant: Amber Orange (representing ITENAS).
  - Secondary/Accent: Medium-Dark Green (representing eco-friendliness and biogas).
  - Backgrounds: Clean whites, light grays, or subtle dark sections for contrast.
- **Target Audience:** ITENAS staff, employees, and the general public (households & industry).
- **Language:** The website copy MUST be in Indonesian (Bahasa Indonesia), elaborating professionally and persuasively on the provided context.

### 2. PAGE STRUCTURE & CONTENT REQUIREMENTS

Please build the landing page with the following sections in order. Elaborate on the provided points to create engaging copywriting.

**Section 1: Hero Section**

- **Headline:** "Turning Biogas Into Clean Energy" (or an engaging Indonesian equivalent).
- **Subheadline:** Introduce NICEGAS as an odorless, safe, and renewable Bio-CNG innovation resulting from ITENAS research to achieve energy self-sufficiency.
- **Primary CTA:** "Pesan Sekarang" (Order Now) - This should trigger a modal or scroll down to the Purchase Request Form.
- **Visual:** A tech-savvy 2D flat illustration of a clean, futuristic energy ecosystem.

**Section 2: The Problem & The Solution**

- **Context:** Highlight the poor management of animal waste in West Java (e.g., in Pangalengan, 19,000 cows managed by 3,500 farmers, but only 25% of waste is properly managed).
- **Solution:** Introduce the ITENAS Waste to Energy Miniplant. Emphasize how it turns organic waste into NICEGAS, solving waste accumulation while providing energy security.

**Section 3: How It Works (Process Flow)**
Design a visual timeline or step-by-step flat-design infographic detailing these 7 stages:

1. Animal & Organic Waste: Collection of biomass (carbon & nitrogen-rich).
2. Biodigester: Anaerobic processing into biogas (CH4: 55-70%).
3. Biogas Holder: Storage unit for raw biogas.
4. Low Pressure Compressor: Boosting pressure from 0.02 - 0.1 bar.
5. Scrubber: Purifying biogas to remove H2S (below 200 ppm).
6. Filter (Clean Biogas): Final filtration (CH4: 92%, CO2: 5-7%) stored at 0.5 barg.
7. High Pressure Compressor: Compressing up to 100+ bar into NICEGAS cylinders.

**Section 4: Features & Specifications (Why Choose NICEGAS?)**

- **Highlight USP:** The fuel of the future, turning waste into cheap energy. Lighter, highly safe, and 100% eco-friendly.
- **Specs:** Available in 2 Liter, 2 kg, 5 kg, and industrial sizes.
- **Pricing/Economy:** Highly affordable at approximately Rp4.000 per kilogram.
- **Impact:** Reduces carbon emissions, creates economic value from waste, and improves farmers' welfare.

**Section 5: Implementation Roadmap & Pilot Project**

- Highlight the field trials by the West Java Provincial Government and ITENAS in three locations: Pangalengan, Lembang, and Parongpong.
- Goal: Testing economic value and accelerating implementation for communities.

**Section 6: Partners & Support (Social Proof)**

- Display placeholder logos or a sleek grid for:
  - Developer: ITENAS Bandung (Mechanical Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Informatics).
  - Funding: Kemdiktisaintek & LPDP (PRPB Program).
  - Government: West Java Provincial Government.
  - Operational Partners: Bandung Equestrian Sport & PT Geo Petro Hinalang.
  - Commercialization (Spin-off): PT Rekayasa Industri Itenas (REKINAS).

**Section 7: Press Release / Launch Event**

- **Event:** ITENAS 53rd Dies Natalis.
- **Date/Location:** Sunday, December 14, 2025, at ITENAS Campus (Jl. PH.H. Mustofa No. 23, Bandung).
- **Key Figures:** Prof. Ir. Meilinda Nurbanasari (Rector), M. Fauzan Adziman (Dirjen Kemdiktisaintek), Herman Suryatman (Sekda Jabar), Ir. Iwan Juwana (Director of PT REKINAS).

**Section 8: Purchase Request Form (Final CTA)**

- A clean, user-friendly form for purchasing or inquiries.
- Fields needed: Name, Unit/Affiliation (ITENAS Staff/Public), Phone Number, Desired Cylinder Size, and Address.
- Submit button styled prominently with the Primary Amber Orange color.

### 3. TECHNICAL OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS

- Provide a fully responsive UI layout.
- Use Next.js app router structure.
- Use Tailwind CSS for styling, strictly adhering to the Amber Orange and Dark Green color scheme.
